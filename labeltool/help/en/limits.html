What it can't do (yet)

QL Label Maker is kept simple on purpose. This is what it can't do, or can't do yet.

  • Designing

    • Black only. It doesn't print red on two-colour tape (DK-22251).
    • Artistic text has one format. Single words in their own font, size, bold, italic or underline: only in a paragraph text (Words in their own format).
    • Barcodes: Code 128, GS1-128, EAN-13 and QR code, no others (Barcodes and QR codes).
    • SVG pictures: text along a line, vertical text, filters, masks and links to other files are missing in the print (Pictures).

  • Printing

    • No serial printing. No running numbers and no printing from a list or table. The date and time can be inserted as a field (Artistic and paragraph text).
    • One label per file. For several identical labels, use Copies.

Missing something?

Please report wishes and bugs on the project page: github.com/joffs/ql-unidrv/issues.
